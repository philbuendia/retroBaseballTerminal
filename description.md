Built around chunky 8-bit sprites and real-time optical pose tracking, this computer baseball game transforms a standard webcam into a full-body arcade cabinet with zero keyboard, mouse, or controller input. The visual design channels late-1970s home console hardware—featuring high-contrast blocks of electric green turf, terracotta dirt, CRT scanline filters, and synthesized square-wave audio—while the computer's camera tracks shoulder rotation, elbow angles, and hand velocity at 60 frames per second.

**Touchless Camera Interface**

* **Silhouette Calibration:** Stepping three to six feet back from the computer monitor aligns the player's body inside an on-screen pixel outline, automatically mapping shoulder width and arm reach.
* **Gesture Navigation:** Raising a left or right hand highlights oversized arcade tiles for Batter or Pitcher mode, while holding a raised hand over a tile for two seconds locks in the selection.
* **Pause and Reset:** Crossing both arms in an "X" across the chest pauses the inning or returns to the mode-select screen without touching the computer.

**Batter Mode Mechanics**

* **Stance and Load:** Standing sideways to the camera and bringing both hands together near the back shoulder cocks the on-screen batter's pixelated bat and triggers the pitcher's windup.
* **Swing Timing and Power:** Swinging both arms across the torso drives the bat through the zone, using real-time hand speed to calculate exit velocity and swing timing to determine pull or opposite-field direction.
* **Launch Angle Control:** The camera tracks the vertical path of the wrists relative to the shoulders, translating a flat swing into a hard line drive and an upward arc into a towering fly ball.
* **Plate Discipline:** Holding the hands back past the torso registers a check swing, while ducking or leaning away dodges inside brushback pitches.

**Pitcher Mode Mechanics**

* **Target Reticle Aiming:** Extending the non-throwing hand moves a blinking 8-bit crosshair around the strike zone, locking the target coordinates once the hand holds steady.
* **Pose-Based Pitch Selection:** Holding both hands at chest level selects a four-seam fastball, raising both arms overhead cues a looping curveball, and flaring the elbows out primes a lateral slider.
* **Delivery and Movement:** Throwing the pitching arm forward toward the webcam launches the ball, where arm speed dictates pitch velocity and the angle of the follow-through bends the ball's horizontal path across the plate.
